## 4. Full Description (App Store & Google Play)

**Break the doomscroll cycle without deleting your favorite apps.**

Ever opened Instagram or YouTube to message a friend, only to lose 45 minutes to an endless feed of Reels or Shorts? You're not alone. Short-form algorithmic video is engineered to capture your attention and keep you hooked.

**Outloop** gives you back your choice. Rather than abruptly locking you out of entire apps, Outloop acts as an intelligent, serene gatekeeper. The moment a short-form video feed begins playing, Outloop steps in with a calming, breathing intervention—breaking the subconscious loop before autopilot takes over.

### ✨ How Outloop Works

1. **Targeted Feed Interception**: Outloop specifically identifies when Reels, YouTube Shorts, or Facebook Reels open, while leaving DMs, feeds, and essential features uninterrupted.
2. **The Mindful Pause**: A tranquil 2-to-4-second breathing pause allows your prefrontal cortex to re-engage. Reflect on your intent before continuing.
3. **Intentional Choices**: Exit directly with a single tap, or proceed with a timed 5-minute mindful pass if you genuinely intended to watch.
4. **Actionable Insights**: Track your daily reclaimed time, avoided loops, and weekly streaks as you rebuild intentional phone habits.

---

### 🔒 100% Private & On-Device

Your privacy is our foundational commitment:
- **Zero Data Collection**: No browsing history, keystrokes, personal messages, or credentials are ever monitored, recorded, or transmitted.
- **Offline Processing**: All detection rules run exclusively on your device hardware using local accessibility APIs.
- **Zero Cloud Accounts**: No logins or remote servers required. Your habit statistics remain locked on your device.

---

### 🌿 Key Features

- **Custom Guarded Apps**: Toggle protection independently for Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, and more.
- **Adjustable Breathing Friction**: Tailor the reset duration (2s quick reset vs. 4s deep breath) to match your workflow.
- **Temporary Breathers**: Take a scheduled 5-minute, 15-minute, or 1-hour mindful pass whenever you need uninterrupted access.
- **Dark & Light Mode**: Serene, eye-friendly aesthetics crafted around the principles of calm, intentional technology.
- **Battery-Efficient**: Engineered with minimal background resource usage so your battery stays full all day.

Reclaim your focus. Take a breath. Step out of the loop with **Outloop**.
