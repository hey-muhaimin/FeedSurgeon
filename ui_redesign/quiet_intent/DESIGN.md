## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
The brand philosophy rests on "Quiet Confidence" — technology engineered to honor human intent rather than harvest dopamine. The visual tone is architectural, grounded, and organic. It balances editorial restraint with physical tactility, avoiding hyper-digital tropes, high-contrast neon alerts, and gamified animations. Every visual component conveys stillness, room to breathe, and intentional Friction: moments where users pause, assess their habits, and choose.

### Target Audience & Emotional Response
Designed for mindful Android power users, knowledge workers, and individuals reclaiming focus from algorithmic loops. The interface evokes grounded clarity, serenity, and purposeful control. Interacting with the product should feel akin to stepping into an acoustically treated library or handling a finely bound physical notebook.

### Design Movement
An organic fusion of **Architectural Minimalism** and **Tactile Warmth**. It eschews stark, clinical white-screen minimalism in favor of structured linen textures, soft parchment-like surfaces, muted mossy greens, and hairline delineations inspired by print drafting.

## Colors

### Palette Rationale
The palette is rooted in earth minerals and botanical undertones, avoiding synthetic saturation. 

- **Primary (`#29362E` - Forest 900):** Provides weight, intentional contrast, and anchor points across primary interactions, app bars, and high-emphasis states. Supported by Forest 800 (`#35463B`) and Forest 700 (`#435A4A`) for active states and focused containers.
- **Secondary (`#A8B3A0` - Sage 500):** Brings quiet equilibrium. Used for secondary toggles, progress tracks, and ambient illustrations. Supported by Sage 300 (`#C8D0C2`) for muted backdrops.
- **Tertiary (`#B18C5A` - Brass 500):** Represents mindfulness triggers and intentional milestones. Applied selectively to focus-break summaries, time tokens, and active intervention rings. Supported by Brass 300 (`#D5BE9C`).
- **Neutral Canvas & Surfaces:** 
  - Canvas Base: Linen 100 (`#EAE8DF`) creates an organic, paper-like underlying environment.
  - Main Component Surface: Ivory 100 (`#FAF9F5`) produces crisp readability without cold blue emission.
  - Secondary Sub-surfaces: Ivory 200 (`#F3F1EA`) provides sunken nesting.
  - Structural Hairlines: Stone 200 (`#E1DED5`) defines boundaries without optical visual noise.
- **Typography & Ink:**
  - High-emphasis text: Ink 900 (`#202720`)
  - Low-emphasis text & captions: Slate 500 (`#777C75`) and Slate 700 (`#505951`)
- **Semantic Functional Accents:**
  - Success: `#287A59` (Surface tint: `#E4F1E9`)
  - Warning / Intervention: `#A97732` (Surface tint: `#F6EEDC`)
  - Critical / Error: `#B84B46` (Surface tint: `#F7E8E5`)

### Color Distribution
Surfaces are 80% Ivory and Linen, 15% Deep Forest and Slate structure, and 5% Brass and Sage accents. Semantic alerts are never allowed to flash across wide screen areas; they reside inside muted soft containers.

## Typography

### Structural Pairing
- **Display, Headlines & Labels (Manrope):** Geometric, calm, balanced, with modern proportions that communicate stability and architectural permanence.
- **Continuous Reading & Body Copy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Soft, open geometric curves that prevent eye strain across long reflection notes, setup walk-throughs, and habit logs.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** Numeric metrics always render with `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1` enabled to keep screen-time counters, countdown timers, and focus sessions vertically aligned without jitter.

### Casing & Tone Rules
- Headings and button text strictly follow sentence case. Title case is disallowed.
- Punctuation in headlines is omitted unless framing an intentional pause (em-dash or terminal period).
- Spacing between lines is intentionally loose (minimum 1.4x font size on body copy) to create an uncluttered, spacious cadence.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy
The screen architecture operates on an 8dp baseline grid with a 4dp micro-step for compact icon and inline label alignments. Layouts use a fluid columnar structure enclosed by generous margins:
- **Mobile (<600dp):** Single column, 4 layout columns, 16dp gutters, and 20dp edge canvas margin.
- **Tablets & Foldables (600dp–840dp):** 8-column layout with 24dp gutters and 32dp outer padding, allowing secondary split sheets and persistent utility rails.
- **Expanded Screens (>840dp):** 12-column layout capped at 1040dp maximum readable container width, framed by 48dp margins.

### Vertical Rhythm
Screen hierarchy emphasizes empty breathing room over dense stacking. Primary surface cards are spaced at least 16dp (`space-md`) apart. Distinct functional sections (such as Daily Limits vs. Intervention Rules) require 32dp (`space-xl`) or 48dp (`space-xxl`) separators to induce an unhurried, contemplative reading flow.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tonal Tiers over Drop Shadows
To preserve a paper-like, quiet aesthetic, synthetic multi-layer drop shadows and dark blur fields are eliminated. Depth is established through **Tonal Layering** and **Hairline Outlines**:

- **Canvas (Level 0):** Linen 100 (`#EAE8DF`). The foundational plane of the Android window.
- **Surface (Level 1):** Ivory 100 (`#FAF9F5`) with a 1dp crisp boundary in Stone 200 (`#E1DED5`). Standard state for cards, lists, bottom sheets, and app bars.
- **Sunken Surface (Level -1):** Ivory 200 (`#F3F1EA`) with an optional inset 1dp hairline. Used for nested input fields, search slots, and disabled inactive modules.
- **Floating Modals & Sheets (Level 2):** Ivory 100 (`#FAF9F5`) resting on an ambient scrim of Forest 900 at 24% opacity (`rgba(41, 54, 46, 0.24)`). For accessibility and edge separation, modals utilize an ambient contact veil: `0 4px 20px rgba(41, 54, 46, 0.06)` combined with a 1dp border of Stone 200.

### Surface Interaction
States do not lift upward along a z-axis. When a user presses a card or interactive element, it shifts tonally to Ivory 200 or Forest 800 (for filled variants) with a 150ms ease-out transition.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Geometry
Shapes are soft, sculpted, and human. The design avoids aggressive pin-sharp corners as well as generic hyper-circular bubbles.

- **Primary Interactive Elements (Buttons & Actions):** 16dp corner radius (`rounded-lg` equivalent in this token system), providing a friendly, tactile pill-like feel without collapsing into an elongated stadium ellipse.
- **Containers & Surfaces (Cards, Modals, Bottom Sheets):** 24dp corner radius (`rounded-xl`), creating an architectural boundary reminiscent of rounded ceramic tiles.
- **Status Tags, Small Chips, and Filter Pills:** Fully circular / pill radii (9999dp) for immediate categorical scanning.
- **Internal Nested Elements (Thumbnails, Icon Well Backdrops):** 12dp corner radius to sustain optical concentricity inside 24dp parent cards.

### Border Execution
All surface borders must be rendered as precise 1dp strokes using Stone 200 (`#E1DED5`). On high-density Android displays, borders remain strictly 1 physical density-independent pixel to preserve fine craftsmanship.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action Button:** Height 52dp, 16dp corner radius. Fill: Forest 900 (`#29362E`), Text: Ivory 100 (`#FAF9F5`), Label Style: `label-lg`. Pressed state: Forest 800 (`#35463B`).
- **Secondary Outlined Button:** Height 52dp, 16dp corner radius. Fill: Ivory 100 (`#FAF9F5`), Border: 1dp Stone 200 (`#E1DED5`), Text: Forest 900 (`#29362E`). Pressed state: Ivory 200 (`#F3F1EA`).
- **Quiet Ghost Button:** Height 44dp, 16dp corner radius. Fill: Transparent, Text: Slate 700 (`#505951`). Pressed state: Ivory 200 with 50% opacity.

### Chips & Filter Tokens
- **Tag / Filter Chip:** Height 36dp, pill-shaped (fully rounded). 
  - Unselected: Surface Ivory 100, 1dp Stone 200 border, text Slate 700.
  - Selected: Surface Forest 900, text Ivory 100, border Forest 900.
  - Accent / Focus: Surface Brass 300 at 30% alpha, text Ink 900, border Brass 500 at 40% alpha.

### Cards & Container Panels
- Standard cards sit on Ivory 100 with 24dp corner radii, surrounded by a 1dp Stone 200 border. Internal padding is strictly 20dp or 24dp (`space-lg`). Content within a card is arranged with a clear headline, a subtle divider in Stone 200 when separating sub-modules, and tabular values cleanly stacked.

### Lists & Row Dividers
- List items feature 56dp minimum touch target heights. Surfaces inside lists inherit the parent container color. Dividers are Stone 200 hairlines inset by 16dp from the left margin to preserve row cohesion while respecting iconography alignment.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Switches
- **Switches:** Android Material You standard dimensions altered to fit the quiet palette. Track: Sage 300 (`#C8D0C2`) when inactive; Forest 900 (`#29362E`) when active. Thumb: Ivory 100 (`#FAF9F5`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 20dp diameter/box, 6dp corner radius on checkboxes. Unselected border: Slate 500. Selected fill: Forest 900 with Ivory 100 check/dot icon.

### Input Fields
- Text input fields rest on Sunken Surface Ivory 200 (`#F3F1EA`) with a 1dp Stone 200 border and 16dp corner radius. Focused state shifts border to Forest 700 (`#435A4A`) with no neon glow rings. Floating labels utilize `label-md` in Slate 700.

### Brand Signature Component: "The Open Loop" Interruption Meter
- A circular progress vector spanning 120dp to 240dp representing session usage or mindfulness boundaries.
- **Visual Anatomy:** Rather than a closed 360° circle, the ring intentionally breaks with a permanent 25° gap at the top right quadrant (representing space for choice and mindful interruption).
- **Styling:** Inactive background track is Sage 300 at 2dp stroke width. Active progress uses Forest 900 or Brass 500 with rounded cap endings, never fully closing into an automated, inescapable loop.
