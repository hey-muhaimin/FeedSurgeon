Android accessibility settings allow Outloop to spot when a short-form video feed opens, with zero network tracking.

How to turn it on

  1. 1

    Tap Open System Settings

    Takes you straight to Android's Accessibility page.

  2. 2

    Tap Downloaded apps

    Usually located near the center of the list.

  3. 3

    Select Outloop

    Switch it ON and tap Allow.
